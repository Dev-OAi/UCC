# Comprehensive Account Opening Checklist: Manager-Managed LLC

**Target Entity:** THC Ventures Commodities 5, LLC
**Manager:** Trog Hawley Capital, LLC
**Signer:** Adam C. Ryan (CEO/MD)

### 1. The "Linkage" Package (Parent to Subsidiary)
Since the entity is managed by another company, the bank needs to see the chain of authority.
*   [ ] **Articles of Organization (Subsidiary):** Filed document for *THC Ventures Commodities 5, LLC*.
*   [ ] **Operating Agreement (Subsidiary):** Must explicitly state that *Trog Hawley Capital, LLC* is the Manager.
*   [ ] **Operating Agreement (Parent):** Must state that *Adam C. Ryan* has the authority to sign on behalf of *Trog Hawley Capital, LLC*.
*   [ ] **Corporate Resolution:** Drafted to name Adam C. Ryan as the authorized signer for the Ventures 5 account.

### 2. Legal & Identification
*   [ ] **EIN Verification:** IRS CP575 or 147C Letter for the *THC Ventures Commodities 5, LLC*.
*   [ ] **Photo ID:** Clear copy of Adam C. Ryan's Driver's License or Passport.
*   [ ] **SSN Verification:** For all beneficial owners or control persons.
*   [ ] **Residential Address:** Personal address for the signer (Banks cannot accept a P.O. Box for this field).

### 3. Beneficial Ownership (FinCEN Compliance)
*   [ ] **Certification of Beneficial Ownership:** Bank form identifying anyone with >25% equity or Substantial Control (Adam C. Ryan).
*   [ ] **Parent Ownership Chart:** If Trog Hawley Capital is owned by a trust or complex family structure, a simple ownership chart helps the bank trace the capital source.

### 4. Business Verification (The "Anti-Red Flag" Pack)
*   [ ] **Business Purpose Statement:** Briefly describing the proprietary investment focus.
*   [ ] **Portfolio Evidence:** Printouts from *troghawley.com* showing the high-profile investments (SpaceX, etc.).
*   [ ] **Office Proof:** Utility bill or lease for 501 Village Blvd (if required for physical presence verification).

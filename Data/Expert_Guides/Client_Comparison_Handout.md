### Why the Advantage Bundle for THC Ventures?

For a specialized investment entity with high-profile assets, **security is the most critical asset**.

*   **Positive Pay:** Acts as a "Digital Security Guard" that stops fraudulent checks and ACH pulls before they ever hit your balance.
*   **Transfer Efficiency:** With unlimited incoming wires, your capital infusions move without being nickeled-and-dimed.
*   **Multi-User Access:** Delegate tasks to your COO or assistant while maintaining 100% control over your primary login and credentials.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** At a $5,000 balance, you get a more secure, more powerful account for **$15 less per month** than a standard utility account.

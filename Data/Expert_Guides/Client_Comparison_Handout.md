### Why the Advantage Bundle (Type 551) for THC Ventures?

For a specialized investment entity with high-profile assets, **security is the most critical asset**.

*   **Advanced Fraud Protection:** Includes **Positive Pay (RPP & ACHPP)**. This acts as a "Digital Security Guard" that stops fraudulent checks and ACH pulls before they ever hit your balance.
*   **Transfer Efficiency:** Unlimited Single ACH Outgoing originations and included Wire/ACH online modules allow for professional, low-cost capital movement.
*   **Multi-User Access:** Delegate tasks to your COO or assistant while maintaining 100% control over your primary login and credentials.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** By maintaining a $5,000 balance, you secure an institutional-grade account for **$15 less per month** than a standard utility account with a wire module.

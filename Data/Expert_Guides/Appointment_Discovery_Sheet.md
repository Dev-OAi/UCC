## 1. Top Discovery Questions (Open-Ended)

### On Cash Flow & Movement:
*   "Beyond the Safe Deposit Boxes, how will this LLC be used for fund movement? Will it primarily be receiving capital from the family office or sending it out for acquisitions?"
*   "What is your typical monthly wire volume (incoming vs. outgoing)? Do you have time-sensitive capital calls that require same-day funding?"

### On Team & Access:
*   "Who else on the Trog Hawley team will need to access this account? Should we set up separate secure logins for your COO or assistant to prepare payments for your final approval?"

### On Risk & Security:
*   "Given the high-profile nature of your portfolio (SpaceX, xAI, etc.), how are you currently protecting your accounts from unauthorized ACH pulls or fraudulent checks?"
*   "Would you find value in having an automated 'Positive Pay' system that blocks any transaction not pre-authorized by your office?"

### On Relationship & Growth:
*   "We noticed your focus on venture and commodities—both of which can have long liquidity cycles. How do you currently bridge the gap between identifying an investment and the actual capital call? Would a future bridge line of credit be something to discuss?"

## 2. Discovery Questions with "Expert Context"

### A. Transaction & Cash Management
*   **Question:** "As you manage commodity-linked contracts and venture investments, do you anticipate a high volume of monthly transactions, or are your movements typically larger, less frequent capital calls?"
*   **Expert Context:** "Understanding your transaction patterns helps me recommend an account that minimizes per-item costs. For example, if you're processing over 500 items, the 510 account is very efficient; however, for complex investment vehicles, our Bundle accounts often provide better value by including the advanced security tools you'll likely need."

### B. Funds Movement (Wires & ACH)
*   **Question:** "For your capital deployments, will you primarily be using domestic or international wire transfers? Also, would having the ability to initiate batch payments (ACH) for distributions or vendor payments be a priority?"
*   **Expert Context:** "Since time-sensitive liquidity is critical in venture and commodity trading, we want to ensure you have the most secure and cost-effective wire platform. Our Advantage and Premier bundles include the Wire Module at no extra cost, providing you with a higher level of control and security for large-dollar movements."

### C. Fraud Mitigation & Security
*   **Question:** "Given the sophisticated nature of your portfolio (SpaceX, Anthropic, etc.), what protocols do you currently have in place to monitor for unauthorized ACH or check activity? Would a 'Positive Pay' system—where you authorize every outgoing payment before it clears—add value to your internal controls?"
*   **Expert Context:** "For family offices, security is often the top priority. Fraudsters frequently target high-net-worth entities. Our Treasury bundles include Fraud Mitigation tools as a standard feature, allowing you to proactively protect your capital rather than reacting to unauthorized charges after they occur."
